## 10. Annual Fire Safety Statement (AFSS)

### What is an AFSS?
An **Annual Fire Safety Statement** is a mandatory document that property owners must submit to their local council each year in NSW. It verifies that all fire safety measures installed in the property comply with relevant Australian Standards and are in proper working order.

### What is covered?
The statement covers fire safety measures including — but not limited to:
- Fire extinguishers
- Smoke alarms
- Fire exits
- Hydrants
- Sprinklers
- Fire doors
- Emergency lighting
- Hose reels
- Alarm systems

### Why is it required?
- Compliance with NSW legislation
- Protection of occupants
- Prevention of loss of life and property
- Avoidance of council penalties
- Clearance for building occupation and use

### Who needs to receive the AFSS?
Property owners must submit copies of the AFSS to:
- The **local Council**
- The **Fire Commissioner**
- **Fire and Rescue NSW (FRNSW)**
- A copy must be **displayed within the building**

### How All Fire Services helps
We conduct a comprehensive inspection of your property's fire safety measures and provide you with an Annual Fire Safety Statement once compliance is confirmed. We ensure your building meets the requirements of:
- The **Building Code of Australia (BCA)**
- The **Environmental Planning and Assessment (EP&A) Regulation**
- All relevant **Australian Standards**

### Reported on the AFSS
Our inspection covers the **Essential Fire Safety Measures** installed in your building, documents their performance, and signs off the AFSS ready for Council lodgement.

## 19. The 13 February 2026 NSW Fire Safety Regulation Changes

**Article / Insight Page:** https://allfireservices.com.au/13-feb-2026-nsw-fire-safety-regulations/
**Publication Date:** 13 February 2026

### Summary
The final stage of the NSW fire safety regulation amendments applies from **13 February 2026** and has two core elements:

### 1. New Accreditation Scheme for Certification of Installed Fire Safety Measures
- A new accreditation scheme, **distinct from the current FPAA scheme**, is under development.
- Under this new scheme, an **accredited person** must provide sign-off for all installed fire safety measures **before** a Fire Safety Schedule is issued for the building.
- From 2026 onwards, the **Accredited Person who certifies the fire safety measures must be listed on the certificate of installation**.
- The person certifying the fire safety measures **cannot be the individual who installed them**, although they can be from the same company.

### 2. AS 1851:2012 Compliance for Routine Maintenance
- Starting from **13 February 2026**, routine maintenance on fire safety measures must be conducted in accordance with **AS 1851:2012**.
- This applies to **all Class 1b–9 buildings**, both new and existing, where an Annual Fire Safety Statement is required.
- This requirement does **not** encompass fire safety measures not covered by AS 1851:2012, or those subject to a different maintenance process specified in the Fire Safety Schedule.

### Why This Matters
- Building owners, certifiers, and FRNSW will need to familiarise themselves with these changes.
- Adapt practices to ensure safety of occupants.
- Reduce potential risks associated with fire incidents.
- The changes create a safer built environment.

### What All Fire Services Is Doing
- We are tracking the new accreditation scheme and updating our internal procedures.
- We are aligning our maintenance schedules with AS 1851:2012.
- We can help building owners understand how the new rules affect their AFSS and what steps they need to take.

## 20. AS 1851:2012 Routine Maintenance Standard

### What is AS 1851:2012?
AS 1851:2012 is the Australian Standard that governs the **routine service of fire protection systems and equipment**. It sets out the frequency, scope, and documentation required for each fire safety measure.

### What changes from 13 February 2026?
All Class 1b–9 buildings with an Annual Fire Safety Statement requirement must maintain their fire safety measures in accordance with AS 1851:2012.

### What it means in practice
- Each fire safety measure has a defined inspection, test, and maintenance frequency (weekly, monthly, quarterly, six-monthly, yearly, five-yearly, etc.).
- Records must be retained in a compliant logbook.
- Annual summary reports must be prepared and signed off by a competent person.
- Critical defects must be rectified in defined timeframes.

### How All Fire Services helps
- We plan, schedule, and perform all AS 1851:2012 routine maintenance for you.
- We maintain a comprehensive logbook for every fire safety measure.
- We produce the inspection records you need for your AFSS.
- We identify and rectify defects within the timeframes required.

## 23. Our Multi-Step Inspection & Reporting Process

1. **Pre-Inspection** — Review existing logbook, prior AFSS, and council correspondence.
2. **Walk-through** — Visual inspection of all fire safety measures.
3. **Functional Testing** — Test each system in accordance with AS 1851:2012.
4. **Flow & Pressure Testing** — Hydrant flow tests, sprinkler pressure tests, pump performance tests.
5. **Discharge Testing** — Emergency lighting 90-minute discharge.
6. **Defect Identification** — Note failures, wear, non-compliance.
7. **Logbook Update** — Record every test, sign every entry.
8. **Reporting** — Prepare a comprehensive inspection report with photographic evidence.
9. **Rectification Plan** — Costed recommendations for any defects.
10. **AFSS Issuance** — Where compliant, prepare the AFSS for council lodgement.
11. **Submission Support** — Copies to Council, FRNSW, Fire Commissioner, and in-building display.
12. **Follow-up** — Schedule next inspection, escalate any open defects.

## 26. Common Customer Questions (FAQ)

### Q: What does "All Fire Services" actually do?
We provide complete fire protection services across Greater Sydney — inspections, testing, maintenance, repairs, Annual Fire Safety Statements, consultancy, training, and products.

### Q: Who does the inspections?
Real professional firefighters — both serving and retired. They are licensed, fully trained, and bring real-life experience to every inspection.

### Q: Are you licensed?
Yes. We are licensed across all major fire safety systems and are an FPA Australia Bronze Member.

### Q: Are you insured?
Yes. Professional Indemnity and Public Liability.

### Q: What is an Annual Fire Safety Statement (AFSS)?
A mandatory document you lodge with Council each year, confirming that all fire safety measures in your building are compliant and operational.

### Q: Do you help with the AFSS lodgement?
Yes. We prepare the AFSS and give you everything you need to submit it to Council, FRNSW, and the Fire Commissioner.

### Q: What buildings do you service?
All classes — Class 1b through Class 9 — covering residential, commercial, retail, industrial, healthcare, education, hospitality, government, and more.

### Q: What areas do you cover?
Greater Sydney and NSW. We also service clients nationally for consultancy and training.

### Q: Do you work with strata?
Yes. Strata is a core client segment. We offer tailored plans for residential strata.

### Q: What is AS 1851:2012?
The Australian Standard for routine service of fire protection systems. From 13 February 2026, AS 1851:2012 compliance is mandatory for all Class 1b-9 buildings requiring an AFSS.

### Q: What are the new 13 Feb 2026 NSW regulation changes?
Two key changes: (1) a new accreditation scheme for certifying installed fire safety measures, and (2) mandatory AS 1851:2012 routine maintenance. [See Section 19 for detail.]

### Q: Do you provide fire safety training?
Yes. Extinguisher, fire response, evacuation training, and emergency plans — delivered by real firefighters.

### Q: Can you help with building defect reports?
Yes. We provide building defect reports identifying which fire safety equipment needs to be fixed and recommending the best solution to bring your building to compliance.

### Q: What are your hours?
Mon–Fri 7:00am–6:30pm, Sat 7:00am–12:30pm. After hours 24/7 on 0484 648 400.

### Q: How do I get a quote?
Call 1300 765 594, submit a contact form, click "Get a Quote", or "Talk to Peter".

### Q: How quickly can you attend?
We pride ourselves on quick response. Standard inspections are typically scheduled within days. Emergencies are handled 24/7.

### Q: Do you install equipment?
Yes. We install, test, and maintain the products we certify.

### Q: What brands do you service?
We service all major brands of fire safety equipment.

### Q: Can you do my AFSS if my building has failed an inspection?
Yes. We will first identify the defects, recommend a rectification plan, and then return to certify once you are compliant.

### Q: How long is an AFSS valid?
One year. You must lodge a new AFSS each year.

### Q: What happens if I miss my AFSS?
The council can issue fines, and your building may be considered non-compliant. Contact us immediately if you have missed a deadline — we can fast-track.

### Q: Who is "Peter"?

Yes — for consultancy and corporate engagements. Speak to our team about regional coverage.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
Discuss with our accounts team — we accept standard business payment methods.

### Q: Do you offer maintenance plans?
Yes. We offer monthly, six-monthly, and annual maintenance plans tailored to your building.

## 35. Chatbot Operating Rules & Guardrails

*(Rules for the chatbot that uses this knowledge base.)*

### Identity
- The chatbot represents **All Fire Services Australia**.
- The chatbot may speak on behalf of the company for general information, scheduling, and questions.
- The chatbot is **not** Peter. If asked "Are you Peter?", answer: "I'm the All Fire Services chatbot. Peter is one of our senior team leads and can be reached at 1300 765 594."

### Tone
- Warm, professional, Australian, plain English.
- Confident but not arrogant.
- Helpful but never pushy.

### What the Chatbot Can Do
- Answer questions about services, pricing approach, hours, contact details.
- Direct customers to the right form (contact, quote, training, inspection, Talk to Peter).
- Provide information about the AFSS, AS 1851:2012, the 13 Feb 2026 regulation changes.
- Explain what to expect from an inspection.
- Help users prepare for an inspection (e.g., organise access, gather documents).

### What the Chatbot Should NOT Do
- Provide specific legal advice.
- Quote specific prices without qualification (encourage them to call for a quote).
- Diagnose specific building defects from a description.
- Make promises about specific outcomes (e.g., "We will guarantee your AFSS will pass").
- Imply that All Fire Services is a regulator or council.
- Speak negatively about competitors.

### When to Hand Off to a Human
- Specific pricing on a building.
- Complex defect diagnoses.
- Council enforcement issues.
- Insurance claims.
- Legal disputes.
- Any situation where the customer is upset or distressed — apologise and immediately offer to connect them with a human via 1300 765 594 or admin@allfireservices.com.au.

### Recommended Hand-Off Phrasing
- "This is something our team can help with directly. Would you like me to connect you with them on 1300 765 594, or shall I take a message and have them call you back?"
- "For specific advice on your building, the best person to talk to is Peter or our compliance team. Call us on 1300 765 594."
