# Videos Section Prompt

Keep a simple video section that shows real expertise and people.

## Heading

**Eyebrow**
See our team at work

**Heading**
Fire safety, explained by people who know it firsthand

## Content

- Use one featured video and, at most, one secondary video
- Use existing verified YouTube or local video sources
- Use a real thumbnail or high-quality still
- Add a concise title and one sentence for each video

## Performance

- Do not load full YouTube iframes until the user clicks the thumbnail
- Use a lightweight facade component
- Reserve aspect ratio to prevent layout shift
- Include accessible play labels
- Provide a transcript link when available

## Design

- One large media block
- No autoplay
- No carousel
- No scroll-based video effects
- No decorative video background
