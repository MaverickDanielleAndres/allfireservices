## Current homepage problems to address

The existing homepage contains too many competing messages, repeated calls to action, repeated “Why All Fire” content, multiple service presentations, several image collections, long logo marquees, large card groups, and overlapping story content.

The current experience asks users to process too much before reaching a clear action. The redesign must reduce choices, shorten scanning time, and create one clear visual story.

## Content inconsistencies to resolve

- The live site contains copy that appears generic or unrelated to fire services.
- Some content repeats on the same page.
- Client logos repeat many times in marquees.
- The services route appears incomplete in the rendered version.
- Some testimonials and names may be placeholders. Do not publish them unless verified.
- Family names, dates, titles, and chronology must be checked against the client-approved source before editing.
- Do not repeat claims that conflict with the December 2009 founding date.

## New homepage narrative

The homepage should answer these questions in order:

1. What does All Fire Services do?
2. Why should I trust them?
3. What makes them different?
4. Who has trusted them?
5. What services can they handle?
6. Can I see the team’s real expertise?
7. What should I do next?

## Pages

### Home

The homepage is the primary conversion page. Keep it concise and complete.

### Our Story

The about page should focus on the family history, firefighter-founded model, authentic team, and business values. Avoid repeating the complete homepage.

### Our Clients

The clients page should focus on verified logos, property types served, verified relationships, and real testimonials or case studies only.

### Contact

Keep the contact form and business information working. Simplify the presentation and remove duplicate CTA blocks where they are unnecessary.
