# Our Services Section Prompt

Simplify the services section so users understand the core offer without reading a catalogue.

## Heading

**Eyebrow**
Our services

**Heading**
Fire safety services that keep your building ready

## Supporting copy

From routine testing and maintenance to Annual Fire Safety Statements, we help property teams stay protected, organised, and compliant.

## Homepage service groups

Show no more than six clear service groups:

1. Annual Fire Safety Statements
2. Testing and routine inspections
3. Fire extinguishers, hose reels, and hydrants
4. Fire panels, alarms, and detection
5. Emergency lighting, exit signs, and fire doors
6. Fire safety compliance and consultancy

Use the exact service labels already approved in the codebase when they differ from this grouping.

## Layout

Preferred options:

- Two-column service list with short descriptions
- Three-column grid with six restrained items
- One large featured service plus a concise service list

Each item should have:

- Clear title
- One sentence
- Optional simple icon
- One direct link

## CTA

- Primary: `Explore Our Services`
- Optional phone link when useful

## Remove

- Multiple separate service sections on the homepage
- Carousels
- Hover-only information
- Inaccurate generic copy
- More than six homepage service cards
- Dense technical details that belong on service pages
