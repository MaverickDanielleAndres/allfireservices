# FAQ Section Prompt

Preserve the existing FAQ questions and verified answers.

## Design

- Simple single-column accordion
- Maximum readable width around 800–900px
- Large tap targets
- Clear plus or chevron indicator
- Strong keyboard and screen-reader support
- Only one subtle transition

## Content handling

- Do not remove important compliance information
- Do not rewrite legal or regulatory claims without verification
- Keep answers concise and link to a relevant detailed page when one remains available
- Add FAQ structured data only when the visible content and schema match exactly

## Remove

- Decorative cards around every question
- Multiple accordion columns on mobile
- Complex animated height effects
- Tiny text
