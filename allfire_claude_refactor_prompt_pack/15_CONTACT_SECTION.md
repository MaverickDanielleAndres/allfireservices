# Contact Section Prompt

Preserve the current form’s fields, validation, submission handler, success state, and error state.

## Suggested heading

**Eyebrow**
Get in touch

**Heading**
Let’s make your building safer

**Supporting copy**
Tell us what your property needs, and our team will help you understand the next practical step.

Keep existing approved copy when the client has requested no text changes.

## Form

Retain these fields when they match the working form:

- Name
- Phone
- Email address
- Suburb
- Message

## Design

- Clear labels above inputs
- Comfortable input height
- Visible focus states
- One-column mobile layout
- Two-column desktop layout only where it improves scanning
- Clear privacy or consent note when required
- Submit button with a specific label such as `Request a Quote` or the existing approved label

## Preserve

- Form integration
- Spam protection
- Error handling
- Analytics events
- Contact email and phone links

Do not turn the form into a multi-step interaction.
