# Our Clients Section Prompt

Create a quiet, credible client section inspired by editorial logo grids, not a continuously moving marquee.

## Heading

**Eyebrow**
Our clients

**Heading**
Trusted by property teams across Sydney

## Supporting copy

We work with strata managers, building owners, facilities teams, and businesses that need fire safety handled clearly, professionally, and reliably.

## Homepage presentation

- Show 6–10 verified client logos in a clean grid
- Keep all logos visually balanced with consistent bounding boxes
- Use monochrome or restrained original colour based on brand approval
- Provide generous spacing
- Add one link: `View our clients`

## Our Clients page

The dedicated page may include:

- Expanded verified logo grid
- Property or client categories served
- Real case studies
- Real, approved testimonials
- One CTA near the end

## Critical credibility rule

Do not use invented client testimonials, fake names, stock portraits, or relationships that are not verified.

If the current page contains unverified testimonials, remove them from rendering and add a content TODO for the client.

## Remove

- Infinite marquee loops
- Multiple repeated copies of the same logo
- Logo cards with unnecessary shadows
- Fake testimonial carousels
- Generic “learn more” cards repeated five times
