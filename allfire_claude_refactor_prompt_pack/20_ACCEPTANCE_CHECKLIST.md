# Final Acceptance Checklist

## Strategy

- [ ] The site feels simple, calm, professional, and brand-aligned
- [ ] A decision-maker can understand the business within five seconds
- [ ] The design is suited to older business owners without feeling dated
- [ ] The website does not look AI-generated

## Header

- [ ] The header uses the latest logo
- [ ] Navigation is limited to core destinations
- [ ] Get a Quote is obvious
- [ ] Mobile menu is accessible
- [ ] The Welcome strapline is removed

## Hero

- [ ] Exact approved hero copy is used
- [ ] Only two primary actions appear
- [ ] Authentic imagery or a clean branded fallback is used
- [ ] No counters, carousel, autoplay video, or complex animation

## Homepage structure

- [ ] Header
- [ ] Hero
- [ ] Our Story
- [ ] The Generations Behind All Fire Services
- [ ] Why All Fire
- [ ] Our Clients
- [ ] Our Services
- [ ] Videos
- [ ] CTA
- [ ] FAQs
- [ ] Our Location
- [ ] Contact
- [ ] Footer
- [ ] No unapproved extra sections

## Story and family

- [ ] Founded by a firefighter copy is present
- [ ] Family section remains prominent
- [ ] Chronology is correct or clearly flagged for verification
- [ ] No names, ages, dates, or roles were invented
- [ ] Authentic family imagery is used when available

## Clients

- [ ] Logos do not repeat in an infinite marquee
- [ ] Only verified clients are shown
- [ ] Fake testimonials and stock portraits are removed
- [ ] Logos have consistent visual sizing

## Services

- [ ] Homepage shows no more than six service groups
- [ ] Duplicate service sections are removed
- [ ] Service copy is relevant to fire safety
- [ ] Important detail remains accessible through clear links

## Preserved sections

- [ ] CTA actions still work
- [ ] FAQ content and behaviour still work
- [ ] Location and map destination remain correct
- [ ] Contact form submission still works

## Visual system

- [ ] Brand colours are used with restraint
- [ ] Typography is consistent
- [ ] Spacing is consistent
- [ ] Card usage is limited
- [ ] Images have consistent treatment
- [ ] Footer is clean and readable

## Routes

- [ ] Home is complete
- [ ] Our Story is complete
- [ ] Our Clients is complete
- [ ] Contact remains functional when retained
- [ ] Extra pages are hidden or handled reversibly
- [ ] Sitemap and navigation match the demo strategy

## Accessibility

- [ ] Keyboard navigation works
- [ ] Focus is visible
- [ ] Headings are logical
- [ ] Images have correct alt treatment
- [ ] Accordions and menus use accessible patterns
- [ ] Form errors are announced clearly
- [ ] Colour contrast is checked
- [ ] Reduced-motion users are respected
