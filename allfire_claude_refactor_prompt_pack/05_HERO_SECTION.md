# Hero Section Prompt

Create a simple, immediately understandable hero that delivers trust within seconds.

## Exact content

**Eyebrow**
Reliable fire safety starts here

**H1**
Fire safety is not a box to tick, it's a responsibility

**Body**
High-level professional fire safety services, delivered by people who are approachable, practical and reasonable. Australian owned and operated since 2009.

**Primary CTA**
Get a Quote

**Secondary CTA**
Explore Our Services

## Optional trust line

Use only one of these below the CTA row:

- Firefighter-founded. Sydney-focused.
- Real experience. Practical protection.
- Protecting people. Protecting property.

## Visual direction

Use one of these approaches, based on authentic assets available in the repository:

### Preferred approach

A split hero with:

- Large editorial copy block
- One authentic photograph of the All Fire team, a technician, or real fire safety work
- Soft rounded image frame
- One restrained brand-colour corner or line detail

### Fallback approach

A strong typographic hero with:

- Warm white background
- Large black headline
- Subtle orange, red, and yellow brand ribbon inspired by supplied branding
- No fake hero photograph
- Reserved slot or TODO note for the authentic Canva image

## Do not include

- Animated counters
- Search forms
- Multiple floating cards
- Video autoplay
- Carousels
- Image zoom effects
- More than two CTAs
- Several competing badges

## Below-fold trust bar

A small trust row may sit directly below the hero when verified assets exist:

- FPA Australia Bronze Member
- Australian owned and operated
- Firefighter-founded
- Serving Greater Sydney

Do not invent accreditation or statistics. Keep the row visually quiet.
