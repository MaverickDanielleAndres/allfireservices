# Our Story Section Prompt

Refactor the homepage story section into a calm editorial introduction that establishes the firefighter-founded difference.

## Exact copy

**Eyebrow**
Our story

**Heading**
Founded by a firefighter

**Paragraph 1**
All Fire Services is an Australian owned and operated business, created by a former NSW Fire Brigades Senior Officer in December 2009.

**Paragraph 2**
The company was founded on a simple idea: that our customer service technicians should be professional firefighters, both serving and retired. It means every client gets extensive, real-life knowledge of the fire safety industry rather than a checklist.

**CTA**
Read our full story

## Recommended layout

- Two columns on desktop
- Copy on one side
- One authentic founder, family, or team image on the other
- On mobile, copy first and image second
- Use substantial white space
- Keep the section to two paragraphs on the homepage

## Optional supporting line

Use one only when it improves the section:

- Fire protection runs in our blood.
- Experience shaped on the fireground, applied to every building.

## Do not include

- Team member carousel
- Coffee story
- Multiple image tiles
- More than one CTA
- Full family timeline inside this section
- Repeated mission, vision, values, and legacy cards
