# Footer Section Prompt

Create a bold but restrained footer inspired by a spacious editorial layout.

## Content groups

- Brand statement
- Core navigation
- Key services
- Contact information
- Business hours
- Social links when active
- Legal links
- Copyright

## Brand statement

Use one of these:

- Protecting people. Protecting property.
- Fire protection runs in our blood.

## Visual direction

- Dark ink background
- White and muted grey text
- Small orange accent
- Optional oversized low-contrast `ALL FIRE` or flame mark as a background wordmark only if it remains readable and does not overwhelm the content
- Generous padding
- Clear columns that collapse cleanly on mobile

## Remove

- Repeated navigation
- Long lists of every hidden service page
- Floating bottom navigation bars
- Excessive badges
- More than one major CTA
